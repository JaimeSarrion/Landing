# Product: jsarrion.es

## What it is
Personal portfolio and CV of Jaime Sarrión, Lead Frontend Developer (Qida, since July 2024), based in Valencia, Spain.
One static page in English.

## Who reads it
- Tech recruiters screening for full-stack or AI-product roles: 30 seconds, want role, years, stack, contact.
- Engineering managers and CTOs: want evidence of judgement — real projects, how AI is used, what was hard.

## The one job
Get an interview for a **full-stack role with an AI focus**. Main action: email Jaime.

- Today: frontend lead (team of five, component library, stack decisions, hiring, mentoring).

- Evidence of AI as product: Torsion (Claude API), planned AI training-plan module in Entrena.

## Tone
Plain, specific, first person, no hype. Facts over adjectives. No "passionate", no skill bars.

## Constraints
- Static output, minimal bytes: no client framework runtime, self-hosted subset fonts, inline SVG.
- Deployed by GitHub Actions to an OVH server from `build/`.
- No phone number on the public page.
